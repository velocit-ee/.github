# Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

Velocitee is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and professional environment for all contributors, maintainers, and community members. We expect collaboration to be respectful, constructive, and grounded in technical integrity.

---

## 2. Expected Standards

We foster an open and collaborative technical community by adhering to the following standards:

### Professional Behavior
* **Constructive Technical Discourse:** Critique ideas, architectures, and implementations based on verifiable technical evidence, benchmarks, and architectural design principles.
* **Supportive Collaboration:** Assist fellow community members with onboarding, troubleshooting, and debugging across diverse operating environments and hardware configurations.
* **Clear Context:** Provide complete environmental details, error traces, and configuration parameters when reporting bugs or requesting assistance.
* **Respectful Engagement:** Treat every contributor with dignity and professionalism regardless of background, technical experience level, age, identity, or organization.

### Unacceptable Behavior
* Any form of harassment, derogatory language, personal attacks, or discriminatory conduct.
* Public or private trolling, insulting commentary, or disrespectful dismissals of inquiries.
* Publishing others' private information (such as physical addresses or unredacted confidential credentials) without explicit authorization.
* Unsolicited commercial advertising, spamming, or disruptive activities across project spaces.

---

## 3. Scope & Enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies within all official project spaces—including GitHub repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, and community discussion boards—as well as whenever an individual officially represents Velocitee in public domains.

### Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers reserve the right to edit, redact, or remove comments, issues, and code submissions that fail to uphold this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently suspend access for repeated or severe violations.

---

## 4. Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it promptly to the maintainers at:

* **Email:** [conduct@velocit.ee](mailto:conduct@velocit.ee) (or [inquiries@velocit.ee](mailto:inquiries@velocit.ee))

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
